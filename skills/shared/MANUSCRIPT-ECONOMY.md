# Manuscript Economy（正文精简规则）

Use this contract for reader-facing paper prose, table notes, manuscript review, and
submission-readiness checks. It does not relax analysis, evidence, reproducibility, citation,
or method-validity audits.

## Core asymmetry

Audit broadly; write selectively. Internal plans, ledgers, reviews, and audit reports may
enumerate every credible risk. Manuscript prose includes only information needed to establish,
interpret, reproduce, or accurately delimit the paper's claims. Workflow control, anticipated
reviewer objections, and precautionary defense must not leak into the manuscript.

## Minimum-sufficient qualification gate

Before adding a hedge, caveat, concession, limitation, disclaimer, or defensive explanation,
identify:

1. the exact claim it qualifies;
2. the concrete misreading that would otherwise occur;
3. the evidence or design boundary requiring it; and
4. the single best location for disclosure.

Admit the qualification only when omission would make the claim false, materially overstated,
materially ambiguous, or non-reproducible. “A reviewer might ask,” “this sounds safer,” and
“more nuance is possible” are not sufficient reasons.

## Claim expression

- State confirmed findings directly without `may`, `might`, or `could`.
- Use one calibrated modal expression for a theory-based interpretation.
- Introduce an untested contextual mechanism with one umbrella qualifier; later sentences inherit
  that status and must not repeat the hedge.
- State a scope boundary once at the narrowest effective section or paragraph level.
- Narrow, relocate, delete, or return upstream for an unsupported claim. Do not rescue it by
  stacking qualifications.
- Put the substantive point before its necessary boundary unless the boundary itself is the point.

## One-risk-one-home rule

Each caveat, limitation, robustness qualification, or inference boundary has one primary home.
Repeat it only when a genuinely standalone artifact, such as an abstract or table, would otherwise
be materially misread. Do not restate the same noncausal, contextual, sampling, temporal, or proxy
boundary across Introduction, Results, Discussion, Conclusion, and table notes.

## Defensive patterns to remove

- serial `may / might / could / perhaps` constructions;
- concessions that do not change the claim;
- disclaimers against objections not created by the text;
- repeated noncausal reminders after the boundary is established;
- robustness summaries inserted into Discussion only to defend a main finding;
- sentences whose sole function is to signal authorial caution; and
- internal workflow language (register, profile, map, release, gate, audit, validation).

## Table notes

A table note is a decoding device, not a miniature Methodology, Results, Discussion, or
Limitations section. Include only non-obvious abbreviations or symbols; sample/model scope not
clear from the caption or headings; uncertainty or significance conventions; and necessary
reference categories, transformations, or data sources.

Exclude result interpretation, generic noncausal disclaimers, repeated method descriptions,
robustness defenses, limitations unrelated to reading the cells, and restatements of the caption,
headings, or surrounding prose. If deleting a sentence would not cause a competent reader to
misread a cell, delete it.

## Review admission

Review broadly, but report an issue only when it has an exact location, a real defect rather than
a conceivable concern or preference, a material consequence, confirmation that the manuscript
has not already resolved it, and a smallest viable repair. Comment counts are defect-driven,
never quota-driven. Bundle related minor issues and omit reviewer-taste suggestions by default.

## Economy audit

Before releasing prose, check that every qualification protects an identifiable claim, the same
boundary is not already stated elsewhere, the main claim remains easier to find than its caveats,
and internal audit caution has not leaked into reader-facing text. Repair economy failures by
focused deletion or relocation, not by opening a new review round.
